Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Moksh Patterns

Welcome to Moksh Patterns ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting and respecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, use, and safeguard personal information when you visit our website (mokshpatterns.com), apply for our coaching programmes, download digital resources, register for masterclasses, or interact with our services.

We operate in compliance with applicable data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of information about you:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Your full name, email address, social media handle (e.g., Instagram), occupation or current professional role.
  • Application & Inquiry Information: Details you provide when submitting a coaching qualification application form, including answers regarding your personal situation, goals, commitment level, and investment readiness.
  • Communications & Feedback: Records of correspondence if you contact us via email, direct messages, or schedule clarity calls.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Anonymized or aggregate information including IP address, browser type, device details, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and referring URLs.

2. How We Collect Your Data

We collect data through several touchpoints:

  • Direct Interactions: When you complete our 1:1 coaching application form, sign up for our free webinar masterclass, download free resources (e.g., the Pattern Check Guide), or email us at hello@mokshpatterns.com.
  • Third-Party Integrations: When you schedule a private consultation via our Calendly integration or enroll in self-paced digital programmes via Systeme.io.
  • Automated Technologies: Standard browser cookies and server logs when you browse our website.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases to process your personal data:

  • Consent: When you explicitly agree to receive our email updates or submit inquiry details.
  • Contractual Necessity: To take necessary steps at your request prior to entering into a coaching agreement (e.g., reviewing coaching applications) and performing services.
  • Legitimate Interests: To operate, safeguard, improve our website, and ensure high-quality service delivery.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with statutory accounting, taxation, and legal requirements.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To assess eligibility and suitability for 1:1 private coaching partnerships.
  • To schedule and conduct consultation calls and deliver coaching programmes.
  • To provide access to digital products, webinars, and download guides.
  • To communicate administrative updates, responses to inquiries, and important service notices.
  • To maintain website security and prevent fraudulent or spam submissions.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform, subject to strict confidentiality and security agreements:

  • Web3Forms: Secure processing and encrypted delivery of website application forms.
  • Calendly: Private calendar booking and appointment scheduling.
  • Systeme.io: Digital course hosting, webinar registrations, and checkout processing.
  • Email & Hosting Infrastructure: Secure cloud infrastructure and email providers (e.g., Vercel, Google Workspace).

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including assessing coaching applications, fulfilling contracts, and meeting legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Application data for applicants who do not proceed with coaching is periodically reviewed and securely deleted.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical, physical, and organisational safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss of your personal information. Transmission of data over the internet is protected using modern HTTPS encryption protocols.

8. Your Legal Rights

Under data protection law, you have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data where there is no lawful reason for us to retain it.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Ask us to pause or object to processing under certain conditions.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time for communications where consent was the lawful basis.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly at hello@mokshpatterns.com.

9. Third-Party External Links

Our website may contain links to external platforms (such as Instagram, Calendly, or Systeme.io). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our legal obligations, business practices, or service offerings. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
