Last Updated: November 2026 • Moksh Patterns

Welcome to Moksh Patterns. We are committed to protecting and respecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used, and protected in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

We collect and process personal data for specific, lawful purposes:

  • Coaching Applications: When you complete an application, we collect your name, email address, professional role, social handle, and information regarding your current challenges and readiness to assess suitability for coaching partnerships and guide you toward the appropriate next step.
  • Webinar Registrations & Free Resources: When you register for a webinar or download resources, we collect your name and email to deliver access, session links, and relevant follow-up content.
  • Digital Purchases: When you enroll in a programme or purchase a digital guide, order and contact details are collected to deliver digital access and maintain statutory transaction records.
  • General Enquiries: When you email us, we collect your contact information and message history to respond to and resolve your enquiry.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK data protection law, we process your information under the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: To take necessary steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (such as assessing coaching applications) and to deliver services or digital products you have requested.
  • Consent: Where you have explicitly opted in to receive marketing communications or updates. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interests: To operate, secure, and improve our website, manage client relationships, and deliver a relevant user experience.
  • Legal Obligation: To maintain required accounting, tax, and company records.

4. Third-Party Services & Tracking

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We work only with select service providers necessary to operate our website and services:

  • Form Processing: Web3Forms processes and delivers application form submissions securely to our email.
  • Consultation Scheduling: Calendly manages appointment scheduling for consultation calls.
  • Digital Delivery & Programmes: Systeme.io hosts our webinars, self-paced programmes, and digital downloads.

Cookies & Analytics: Our website does not use non-essential tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or marketing analytics tools. Only essential technical functions required for site operation and security are active.

5. Form Routing Disclosure

When you submit a coaching application form, your responses help automatically direct you to the most relevant next step (such as scheduling a call or viewing alternative programme options). This routing is intended solely to streamline your navigation and does not involve automated decision-making producing legal effects.

6. Marketing Communications

If you have opted in to receive marketing emails or updates, you may unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link included at the bottom of any email or by contacting us at hello@mokshpatterns.com. Once unsubscribed, you will no longer receive marketing communications.

7. International Data Transfers

Where our third-party service providers store or process data outside the United Kingdom, we ensure appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses or UK International Data Transfer Agreements) are in place to ensure your personal data receives an equivalent level of protection.

8. Data Retention Periods

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected:

  • Unsuccessful Coaching Applications: 6 months after the last interaction.
  • Website Enquiries: 6 months after the enquiry has been resolved.
  • Webinar Registrations: 12 months after the last interaction, unless another lawful basis justifies continued retention.
  • Coaching Client Records: 3 years after the coaching relationship ends, unless longer retention is legally justified.
  • Purchase & Accounting Records: 6 years from the end of the relevant company financial year, in accordance with statutory accounting and tax obligations.
  • Marketing Subscribers: Retained while valid marketing permission exists, subject to periodic reviews.

9. Your Legal Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The right to request erasure of your data where no lawful ground for continued processing exists.
  • The right to restrict or object to the processing of your data under certain conditions.
  • The right to data portability.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@mokshpatterns.com.

10. Right to Complain

If you have concerns about our use of your personal data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by telephone on 0303 123 1113.
