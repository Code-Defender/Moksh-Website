MOKSH PATTERNS — BRAND DIRECTION

BRAND NAME:
Moksh Patterns

CORE BRAND CONCEPT:
WHO TAUGHT YOU THAT?

PRIMARY AUDIENCE:
High-performing Asian women, including leaders, professionals and founders.

CORE THEMES:
- Learned patterns
- Self-trust
- Decision-making
- Self-doubt
- Family expectations
- Cultural expectations
- Identity
- The "good Asian daughter"
- Self-awareness
- Nervous system regulation
- Choosing differently

CORE LANGUAGE:
"Who Taught You That?"
"Recognise. Question. Reclaim."
"We Identify. We Understand. We Break."

VISUAL PERSONALITY:
- Premium
- Editorial
- Sophisticated
- Introspective
- Warm
- Feminine but not overly feminine
- Intelligent
- Personal
- Culturally aware

PRIMARY COLOR:
Deep forest green

SECONDARY COLOR:
Deep charcoal

ACCENT COLOR:
Muted antique gold

LIGHT COLOR:
Warm cream / ivory

TYPOGRAPHY:
Elegant editorial serif for major headlines.
Clean modern sans-serif for body text and UI.

DESIGN ELEMENTS:
- Thin gold divider lines
- Small uppercase gold labels
- Editorial typography
- Warm cinematic photography
- Dark green environments
- Botanical details
- Fine-line decorative elements
- Generous whitespace
- Large typography
- Premium portrait photography

PHOTOGRAPHY:
Warm, cinematic, sophisticated interior environments.
Dark green surroundings.
Natural but confident portraits.
Editorial rather than corporate.

AVOID:
- Generic coaching website design
- SaaS/startup aesthetics
- Bright purple or pink
- Neon colors
- Glassmorphism
- Excessive gradients
- Generic stock photography
- Cartoon illustrations
- Excessive rounded cards
- Excessive animations
- Corporate website aesthetic
- Generic "unlock your potential" messaging

OVERALL DESIGN GOAL:

The website should feel like Moksh's existing Instagram visual identity translated into a sophisticated premium editorial website.

Do not copy Instagram posts literally.

Preserve the same visual language, typography hierarchy, color relationships, photography style, gold detailing, editorial composition and emotional atmosphere.
