3. RECOGNITION SECTION

Now we make the visitor recognize herself.

Small label

THE PATTERNS

Heading
You learned how to succeed.

Then explain the tension between external success and internal expectations.

Use the themes we know from her content:

You learned how to be responsible.
You learned how to meet expectations.
You learned to consider everyone else's opinion.
You learned to question yourself.
You learned to be the "good daughter."

Don't turn these into unsupported claims about every client. Present them as reflective prompts.

For example:

Whose expectations are you carrying?

Whose voice are you listening to?

What have you learned to believe about yourself?

This section should feel like an editorial magazine spread.

4. THE BIG QUESTION

This should be a visually dramatic section.

Dark green background.

Gold line.

Huge serif:

Who taught you that you had to be this way?

Then a short paragraph explaining the philosophy.

The visitor should pause here.

This is where the brand differentiates itself.

5. THE APPROACH

THE WORK

Recognise. Question. Reclaim.

WE IDENTIFY. WE UNDERSTAND. WE BREAK.

This uses the established brand language.
